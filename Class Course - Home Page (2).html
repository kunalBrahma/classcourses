ℹ️ What is Class Course?

Class Course is an online learning platform for students from Class 2 to Class 12 in Assam. Every subject has chapter-wise textbook Q&A, extra advanced Q&A, and practice MCQs. Students can test their knowledge through quizzes, earn coins, and use those coins to get a discount on premium notes. Education videos and the latest education-related news are also available in one place — so students don't need to go anywhere else to study.
